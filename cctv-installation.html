CCTV Installation & Surveillance Systems

Protect what matters most with professionally designed and installed CCTV systems.

We provide end-to-end CCTV installation for homes, offices, shops, schools, and industrial sites. From the initial site survey to final configuration, our certified technicians design a surveillance solution tailored to your property — covering blind spots, entry points, and high-risk areas.

The quality of our installations and the neatness of our work is what earned us clients' trust. We work with trusted camera brands and ensure every system is fully tested before handover.

What's Included

Free site survey & camera placement planning

HD & IP camera installation (indoor & outdoor)

DVR / NVR setup and configuration

Remote viewing on your smartphone or PC

Night vision & motion detection setup

Cable management & tidy finishing

Ongoing maintenance & technical support

Why Install CCTV?

Deter break-ins and vandalism, keep 24/7 records of everything that happens on your property, monitor remotely while away, and provide clear evidence whenever an incident occurs — all from one reliable system.
